Risks

  • Your whole read becomes the contract: the tradeoffs and effort the page used to spell out must be legible in the contract, or a boundary you would have questioned passes unread.
  • The independent read returns findings and writes no record, so nothing outside the contract shows that the read happened or what it found.
  • Legacy and new paths diverge in the gates; a wrong presence test on brief-sha256 can accept an unapproved legacy change or refuse a valid briefless one.
  • Brief-derived metrics stop for new changes, so measurement continuity breaks until the control center decides a replacement.
  • CK5 and CK8 are text and tracked-file scans: guidance elsewhere, and a reference in prose to a deleted document, can still point at the retired brief without failing either.
  • The parser stays for legacy records while the documents that define the format leave the tree, so the legacy path is exercised only by old records and can drift untested.
  • This change removes its own artifact: after it closes no revision of a later change gets a page, so a future material revision relies on the contract and its difference being legible there.
